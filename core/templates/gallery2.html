Meet the Author

Michelle Nicole Benjamin-Layne

A Voice for Generational Restoration

Michelle Nicole Benjamin-Layne is a visionary force behind Manifest Destiny Ministries International and the architect of the global Matriarch Mantle movement. With decades of ministry service, leadership and community engagement, she is committed to equipping women and families to recognise their influence, confront destructive patterns and build a legacy rooted in faith, purpose and truth.

Through teaching, mentorship and spiritual leadership, she calls a generation of women to lead with courage, wisdom and responsibility—at home, in the church and in their communities.
