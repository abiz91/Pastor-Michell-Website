Ethiopia — Tigray

Her Dignity Matters

Through Matriarch Mantle Global Outreach Initiatives (M²OI), we are working to establish Her Dignity Matters in Tigray, Ethiopia—an initiative centered on restoring dignity, providing practical care, and standing with women and girls whose lives have been deeply affected by conflict, displacement, poverty, and gender-based violence.

Women in conflict-affected regions of Ethiopia continue to face serious humanitarian and protection needs. Our desire is not simply to send supplies from a distance. We are building trusted relationships with people who understand the communities, know the women, and are connected directly to the places where help is needed most.

Our Ministry Partner

Pastor/Prophetess Meseret Amare

Pastor/Prophetess Meseret Amare, Pastor of Victorious Church of Christ in Takoma Park, Maryland, serves as an M²OI Global Outreach partner helping connect our ministry with sisters in need in Tigray.

Prophetess Meseret is a well-known Ethiopian prophetess whose ministry and longstanding relationships provide an important bridge between our work in the United States and trusted relationships within Ethiopia.

Through her connections, M²OI is being introduced to individuals and ministry relationships on the ground and near the point of need in Tigray. This allows us to move carefully and relationally— listening first, identifying actual needs, and developing support that is culturally respectful, locally informed, and responsive to the women themselves.

What Her Dignity Matters Seeks to Provide

Practical, survivor-centered support that protects dignity.

As the Tigray initiative develops, our focus is on practical, survivor-centered support that protects dignity rather than reducing women to what they have endured.

  • Feminine hygiene products and dignity kits
  • Soap and personal-care items
  • Undergarments, towels and basic necessities
  • Clothing and essential household support where needed
  • Connections to trusted local resources
  • Compassionate care for women rebuilding after displacement, violence and trauma

Where appropriate and possible, we also hope to support pathways toward emotional and spiritual care, stabilization, community restoration, and longer-term empowerment through trusted partners already serving women locally.

More Than Relief — Restoration

Her Dignity Matters is not built around what happened to a woman. It is built around who she remains.

She is a woman of value. She is worthy of care. She is worthy of safety. She is worthy of dignity. She is worthy of a future beyond what she has survived.
